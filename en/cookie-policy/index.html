Use of cookies

Access to this website may involve the use of cookies. Cookies are small amounts of information stored in the browser used by each user, across the different devices they may use to browse, so that the server can remember certain information that will later be read only by the server that implemented it.

Cookies make browsing easier and more user-friendly and do not damage the device. They can also help recognize the user, personalize the experience, improve use of the website and identify or resolve errors.

Information collected through cookies may include the date and time of visits, pages viewed, time spent on the website and sites visited immediately before or after. No cookie can contact the user by phone or other personal contact means, extract information from the hard drive or steal personal information.

Cookies that make it possible to identify a person are considered personal data and are subject to the Privacy Policy. Their use requires the user's consent, based on an authentic, affirmative, prior, removable and documented choice.

First-party cookies

First-party cookies are sent to the user's computer or device and managed exclusively by ATELAHOMES for the proper functioning of the website. The information collected is used to improve the quality of the website, its content and the user experience.

Type Purpose Status
Necessary technical cookies Allow the website to work and remember the user's cookie choice. Always active
Analytics cookies Would help understand website usage and improve quality. Only if accepted by the user

The website will not activate optional cookies before the user has given consent. The user can accept, reject or configure cookies from the initial banner and can reopen settings from the footer link.

Social media cookies

ATELAHOMES may include social media plugins or links allowing access to those platforms from the website. In that case, social media cookies may be stored in the user's browser. Those social networks have their own data protection and cookie policies and are responsible for their own files and privacy practices.

Disable, reject and delete cookies

The user can disable, reject and delete cookies, in whole or in part, through their browser settings, including Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge. Procedures may vary from one browser to another, so users should consult the instructions provided by their browser.

If the user rejects cookies in whole or in part, they may continue using the website, although some features may be limited.

Changes to this cookie policy

This Cookie Policy may change or be updated. Users are advised to review this page whenever they access the website to remain informed about how and why cookies are used.
